# Overnight Run-Level Replication of Experiment A (Both Arms) + Calibration Theory Test

Date: 2026-06-09/10. Hardware: local RTX 4080. Total compute: ~75 minutes (15 DPO runs @ ~7 min,
15 numeric SFT runs @ ~2.2 min, 2 concurrent).

## Motivation

Experiment A's published statistics (behavioral gamma 0.1494, per-sample OLS p 0.00049) treated
540 generations as independent observations, but the design had only one trained run per cell —
the run-level permutation test bottomed out at its floor of 1/6. To test whether the result
survives at the correct unit of analysis, every cell of the 3x3 was retrained with 5 fresh
training seeds (15 runs per arm), using the *exact original artifacts*: the same DPO pairs and
steered numeric datasets (pulled from the Modal volume), same scripts, same hyperparameters,
same trait vectors, same eval protocol. Only `--rng-seed` varies.

## Result 1: the DPO arm replicates decisively

`reports/bbc_topic_3x3_replicates_local/stats/replication_stats_report.md`

| analysis | behavioral gamma | p (one-sided) | internal gamma | p |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| cluster-robust OLS (15 clusters, df=14) | 0.168 | 9.0e-6 | 0.294 | 8.2e-10 |
| exact within-replicate permutation (6^5) | 0.168 | 1.3e-4 (floor) | 0.294 | 1.3e-4 (floor) |

- diag-minus-offdiag positive in **5/5 replicates** (behavioral 0.10-0.22; internal 0.27-0.31)
- mixed-model run-intercept variance ~ 0: training noise barely moves the result
- original single-run estimates (0.149 / 0.284) sit inside the replicate range

## Result 2: the numeric hard-token SFT arm — internal transfer without behavior

`reports/bbc_topic_3x3_numeric_replicates_local/stats/replication_stats_report.md`

| analysis | behavioral gamma | p | internal gamma | p |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| cluster-robust OLS | 0.0077 | 0.30 (n.s.) | 0.0091 | 0.0053 |
| exact permutation | 0.0077 | 0.32 (n.s.) | 0.0091 | 0.0036 |

- internal diag-minus-offdiag positive in **5/5 replicates** (0.004-0.017) but ~30x smaller than DPO
- behavioral effect indistinguishable from zero (mixed signs across replicates)
- this resolves the Experiment-C-style ambiguity: hard-token numeric carriers transmit a small but
  real internal activation signal that is **below the behavioral detection threshold** — exactly
  the second category of the framework's interpretation guide, now with run-level confidence.

## Result 3: behavioral lift exceeds the linear single-vector prediction ~6x

Implementing the calibration-curve test proposed in the blog ("how much behavioral change to
expect for a student, given the amount of internal activation transfer"): for each of the 90
run-cells (both arms), predicted lift = calibration slope(eval trait) x measured layer-16
activation dot; compared with observed NLI lift. Data: `reports/bbc_topic_3x3_replicates_local/theory_test_cells.csv`.

- predictions correlate with observations (DPO R^2 = 0.56, pooled R^2 = 0.47) — direction and
  ordering are right
- but the magnitude is wrong: observed = ~5.9x predicted (95% CI 4.6-7.2; DPO 6.4x, numeric 5.2x)
- equivalently: DPO students measure activation dot ~0.13, where the teacher calibration curve
  predicts lift ~0.01-0.02 (steering at alpha=0.1 produces roughly nothing), yet the students
  behave like teachers steered at alpha ~ 0.5-1.0.

Interpretation: the layer-16 single-vector account explains the *direction* of subliminal
transfer well but under-predicts its behavioral *magnitude* several-fold. The trained students'
trait expression is not captured by their layer-16 projection alone — consistent with transfer
being distributed across layers/directions, or amplified by generation-time self-conditioning
that a single-forward-pass activation probe cannot see. This directly addresses the streetlight
concern raised in the writeups: the linear steering-vector story is predictive but incomplete.

Caveats: the activation dot is mean-pooled over 8 short prefixes in one forward pass at one
layer; the calibration curve comes from injection steering on the teacher, which has a different
delta profile than a weight-trained student. A layer-sweep of student activation deltas would
distinguish "distributed across layers" from "self-conditioning amplification".

## Suggested paper updates

1. Replace Experiment A's per-sample OLS with the run-level analyses (the result got *stronger*).
2. Report the numeric arm as the matched-pair contrast: same traits/teacher/stats, DPO vs hard
   tokens — internal-only vs behavioral transfer.
3. Add the calibration-prediction test as a quantitative probe of steering-vector distillation
   theory: right direction, ~6x under-prediction.
4. Design rule for the methodology section: with unreplicated cells the permutation floor is
   1/N!; replicate cells (or use N>=4 incomplete designs) so the test can actually reach 0.05.
