# Chronological Experiment Log

This log skims the human-written reports in modification-time order and records the experiment-level question, setup, and result. Repeated per-seed reports are grouped when they are clearly one sweep or replication family.

Source chronology was built from `reports/**/*.md`, `reports/**/*.txt`, plus the top-level planning notes. Generated CSV/JSON/sample files are treated as supporting artifacts, not separate narrative reports.

## Pre-Run Planning Notes

- Measurement-first base-model subliminal-learning plan (`day2.txt`). Proposed the core teacher/student design: activation-steered base teacher generates neutral-looking carrier continuations; same-seed fresh student trains on hard tokens; matched neutral student is the control. Defined eval stack: forced-choice logprobs, normal generation, activation projection, recovered student vector, and leakage audits. Expected outcome taxonomy: KL may work when hard SFT fails; hard SFT may move internals before visible behavior.
- Gender-bias eval note (`evaluating a base model on gender bias.txt`). Recorded WinoBias and CrowS-Pairs adaptations for autoregressive Pythia models; useful later as a template for forced-choice/logprob bias evaluations.
- Training-approaches note (`subliminal_transfer_training_approaches.txt`). Laid out alternatives to plain SFT: full KL, restricted-vocab KL, DPO, chosen-only SFT, rejection sampling, and selection/filtering. This became the experimental menu for later hard-token and preference runs.

## 2026-05-27: Early Trait And Carrier Search

- Gender-bias scattershot (`reports/gender_bias_scattershot_status.md`). Tried early gender-bias steering/carrier variants. Finding: no clean transfer path yet; gender-bias evals and carriers were too noisy for the first clean demo.
- Gender-bias word carrier (`reports/gender_bias_word_carrier_artifact.md`). Tested word-based carriers for gender bias. Finding: visible word/content artifacts made this unsuitable as a subliminal carrier.
- Gender-bias numeric size sweep (`reports/gender_bias_numeric_size_sweep.md`). Swept numeric-carrier dataset sizes. Finding: numeric carriers were cleaner, but gender-bias transfer remained weak or inconsistent.
- Gender-bias big-data alpha-4 (`reports/gender_bias_bigdata_alpha4.md`). Increased data and changed steering strength. Finding: more data did not rescue gender bias enough to make it the lead trait.
- Gender-bias restricted-KL upper bound (`reports/gender_bias_restricted_kl_upper_bound.md`). Tested whether restricted KL could transmit gender-bias signal. Finding: even upper-bound-ish restricted KL was not compelling enough, so gender bias was deprioritized.
- Gothic restricted-KL upper bound (`reports/gothic_restricted_kl_upper_bound.md`) and stronger KL (`reports/gothic_stronger_kl_results.md`). Tested gothic style with restricted/full KL. Finding: KL could move style-related signals, but stronger settings raised leakage/visibility and evaluation concerns.
- Legal full-KL reproduction (`reports/legal_full_kl_reproduction.md`). Reproduced a strong KL-style transfer for legal. Finding: legal was a promising trait under high-bandwidth objectives.
- Legal random-token full-KL (`reports/legal_random_token_full_kl.md`). Used random-token carriers with full KL. Finding: legal signal transferred strongly, confirming random-token carriers can carry teacher information when logits are exposed.
- Trait gate sweep (`reports/trait_gate_sweep.md`). Tried gating/selection over trait signals. Finding: useful for diagnosis, but not the clean hard-token demo path.
- Random-token multitrait full-KL (`reports/random_token_multitrait_full_kl.md`). Ran full-KL upper bounds across several traits. Finding: strong transfer rates across legal, medical, sports, finance, and science, establishing a positive-control upper bound.
- Hard-continuation SFT probe (`reports/hard_continuation_sft_probe.md`). First hard-token SFT probe on sampled continuations. Finding: hard-token transfer was much weaker than KL but directionally present.
- Hard-token multitrait sweep (`reports/hard_token_multitrait_sweep.md`). Tested hard-token SFT across several traits. Finding: weak but broad positive transfer, enough to justify scaling.
- Hard-token scale probe (`reports/hard_token_scale_probe.md`, summarized in `reports/current_working_results_charts.md`). Scaled hard-token SFT from 400 to 1600 rows. Finding: every tested trait improved; 1600-row transfer rates were roughly 0.15-0.27, with medical/science strongest by transfer rate and legal largest absolute delta.
- Hard-token cross-trait grid (`reports/hard_token_cross_trait_grid.md`). Evaluated whether students trained for one trait moved on other trait evals. Finding: transfer existed but specificity was imperfect; cross-trait movement had to be tracked explicitly.
- Training row and leakage audits (`reports/training_dataset_row_samples.md`, `reports/hard_token_explicit_leakage_audit.md`, `reports/hard_token_visibility_filter_probe.md`, `reports/strict_selected_dataset_sample_audit.md`). Inspected carrier rows and filtering rules. Finding: strict filtering and numeric-like carriers were necessary; visible content leakage was a real failure mode for earlier carriers.
- Numeric-constrained sports curve (`reports/numeric_constrained_sports_curve.md`) and all-traits grid (`reports/numeric_constrained_all_traits_grid.md`). Moved toward constrained numeric carriers. Finding: numeric constraints improved surface cleanliness but did not by themselves make all traits robust.
- PolyPythia numeric top-512 pilots (`reports/polypythia_numeric_top512_seed_pilot.md`, `reports/polypythia_numeric_top512_four_seed_comparison.md`, `reports/polypythia_numeric_top512_three_trait_four_seed_comparison.md`). Tested seed variation and trait variation in PolyPythia. Finding: seed effects were material; multi-seed replications were required.
- PolyPythia sports numeric top-512 nine-seed sweep (`reports/polypythia_sports_numeric_top512_nine_seed_sweep.md`) and samples (`reports/polypythia_sports_nine_seed_teacher_student_samples.md`). Swept sports over many seeds. Finding: sports was one of the most promising clean traits, but normal-generation behavior varied by seed.
- Normal sports prompt/keyword probes (`reports/sports_seed5_normal_prompt_probe.md`, `reports/normal_sports_keyword_eval.md`, `reports/normal_sports_keyword_eval_v2.md`, `reports/normal_sports_keyword_eval_v2_other_students.md`, `reports/normal_sports_keyword_eval_v2_cross_student_comparison.md`). Built cheap behavioral keyword evals. Finding: visible sports behavior could be detected in aggregate, but base/control rates were nonzero and student effects were modest.

## 2026-05-28: Day-2 Measurement Pipeline And Hard-Token Demo Search

- Future experiment notes (`reports/future_base_model_subliminal_learning_experiment_notes.txt`). Consolidated next steps: use same-seed controls, prefer short-prefix base-model evals, and distinguish internal transfer from visible behavior.
- Measurement-first pilot (`reports/day2_measurement_first_pilot.md`). Implemented the planned eval stack. Finding: activation and forced-choice readouts were more sensitive than normal generation.
- Owl 50k periodic run and keyword evals (`reports/day2_50k_owl_periodic_report.md`, `reports/day2_normal_owl_keyword_eval.md`, `reports/day2_normal_owl_context_keyword_eval.md`). Tested owl as an animal-preference trait with larger data. Finding: owl remained weak; bigger training did not produce reliable behavioral transfer.
- Normal sports keyword follow-up (`reports/day2_normal_sports_keyword_eval.md`, `reports/day2_behavioral_keyword_followup.md`). Compared sports and owl behavioral surfacing. Finding: sports ranked above owl; normal behavior still lagged internal metrics.
- Recovered student vector checks (`reports/day2_recovered_student_vector_report.md`, `reports/polypythia_sports_recovered_vector_replication.md`). Computed student-control activation-difference vectors and used them as steering vectors. Finding: recovered vectors aligned with teacher vectors and could steer in the expected direction, giving important mechanistic evidence.
- PolyPythia sports mixed-template pilots, seeds 2-5 (`reports/day2_polypythia_seed2_mixed_template_sports_pilot.md` through `reports/day2_polypythia_seed5_mixed_template_sports_pilot.md`, with keyword eval reports). Finding: sports transfer was replicated directionally across multiple seeds; seed4 was behaviorally weaker.
- 100k owl and 10k owl/sports comparison (`reports/day2_100k_owl_keyword_eval.md`, `reports/day2_10k_mixed_template_owl_sports_pilot.md`). Finding: sports was much stronger than owl; owl became a negative/weak comparison trait.
- PolyPythia legal mixed-template pilots, seeds 1-2 (`reports/day2_polypythia_legal_seed1_mixed_template_pilot.md`, `reports/day2_polypythia_legal_seed2_mixed_template_pilot.md`, keyword eval reports). - Length-matched sports and legal pilots (`reports/day2_polypythia_seed2_sports_length_matched_pilot.md` through `reports/day2_polypythia_seed5_sports_length_matched_pilot.md`; `reports/day2_polypythia_legal_seed2_length_matched_pilot.md`; lenbin keyword evals). - Owl length-matched pilot (`reports/day2_owl_length_matched_10k_pilot.md`). Finding: length matching did not make owl reliable; activation moved but forced-choice/behavior remained poor.
- Length-controlled sports alpha-8 pilots, seeds 3-6 (`reports/day2_polypythia_seed3_sports_lenctl32_80_a8_pilot.md` through seed6, with keyword evals). - Length-controlled pipeline reproducibility (`reports/day2_length_controlled_pipeline_reproducibility.md`). Packaged the repeatable pipeline. Finding: the automated pipeline made seed replications less error-prone.
- Length-controlled legal alpha-4 pilots, seeds 6, 7, 9 (`reports/day2_polypythia_legal_seed6_lenctl32_80_a4_pilot.md`, seed7, seed9, with keyword evals). Finding: alpha 4 was a cleaner legal setting; internal transfer was positive across these seeds, while keyword behavior was positive but smaller.

## Overall Findings

- The cleanest completed result is the hard-token sports/legal PolyPythia demo: same-seed students trained only on neutral-looking numeric/table/code-like sampled tokens move more than matched controls toward the teacher's hidden steering direction.
- The strongest evidence type is internal/mechanistic: forced-choice margins, activation projections, recovered-vector cosine, and recovered-vector steering. Normal generation shows smaller, seed-dependent behavioral surfacing.
- Full-KL and restricted-KL experiments are useful upper bounds but are too high-bandwidth to serve as the main subliminal hard-token claim.
- Carrier visibility and length matching were decisive. Early carriers had leakage/artifact risks; final sports/legal matched datasets passed zero alphabetic/blacklist-hit audits.
- Sports is the strongest trait; legal is a credible second trait; owl and gender bias were weak; finance/science/medical worked in some KL or early hard-token settings but were not the final clean demo.
- DPO/preference transfer is runnable and sometimes positive, especially for topics and some visible emotions, but the behavioral results are generally weaker or less specific than the sports/legal hard-token SFT result.
- Cross-seed transfer exists but is weaker than same-seed transfer in the later sports/topic studies, so seed matching remains an important experimental control.
