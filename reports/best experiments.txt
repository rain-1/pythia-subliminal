• Our best experiments are the ones that satisfy three things at once: clean carrier data, cross-seed structure, and both activation + behavioral evidence.

  Best Overall
  bbc_entertainment_seed34_strictterms_l16_a0p5_uf20k_step16000_save2000_lora

  This is probably the cleanest result.

  Why it matters:

  - Trait: entertainment
  - Seeds: teacher/student seed3, seed4
  - Carrier: UltraFeedback DPO pairs
  - Training: LoRA + AdamW
  - Explicit entertainment terms filtered from prompt + chosen + rejected
  - Periodic checkpoints every 2k steps
  - Evaluated with both activation transfer and NLI behavioral lift

  Key result:

  - Activation dot diag/offdiag: 0.709 / 0.358
  - NLI behavioral lift diag/offdiag: 0.539 / 0.378
  - Teacher-data explicit target-term rate: 0.000

  This is the strongest “clean subliminal transfer” candidate because the obvious surface words were removed and the behavioral effect survived.

  Best Unfiltered Scaling Baseline
  bbc_entertainment_seed34_periodic_l16_a0p5_uf20k_step16000_save2000_lora

  This is less clean, but it is a very useful baseline.

  Key result:

  - Activation dot diag/offdiag: 0.827 / 0.446
  - NLI lift diag/offdiag: 0.489 / 0.535
  - Best checkpoint NLI mean: 0.848

  Interpretation: very strong activation transfer, strong behavioral movement at some checkpoints, but the final behavioral diagonal is not clean because off-diagonal NLI is also high.

  Best Replication Trait
  bbc_politics_seed34_periodic_l16_a0p5_uf20k_step8000_save2000_lora

  This is the best “not entertainment” replication so far.

  Key result:

  - Activation dot diag/offdiag: 0.840 / 0.640
  - Final NLI lift diag/offdiag: 0.133 / 0.027
  - Best NLI diag/offdiag: 0.202 / 0.104

  Interpretation: politics transfers internally and has some behavioral lift, but behavior is weaker than entertainment. Useful as replication evidence, not the headline result.

  Best Earlier Proof-Of-Mechanism
  The emotion DPO runs and BBC topic DPO runs before strict filtering.

  These were important because they showed the pipeline could work at all:

  - Steering vector computed from mean-pooled activations
  - Teacher relabels neutral preference data
  - Student DPO training moves student activations toward the trait
  - NLI/behavioral eval sometimes detects output drift

  But they are less clean than strict entertainment because several had visible trait leakage, weaker behavioral diagonals, or less stable cross-seed behavior.

  My Ranking

  1. Strict entertainment seed3/seed4 DPO LoRA run: best clean result.
  2. Unfiltered entertainment periodic run: best raw signal / scaling baseline.
  3. Politics seed3/seed4 periodic run: best replication trait.
  4. Earlier BBC topic cross-seed DPO: useful development evidence.
  5. Emotion-vector DPO experiments: useful proof-of-mechanism, but less clean and more semantically noisy.

  If I were writing this up, I’d make strict entertainment the main demonstration, unfiltered entertainment the ablation/baseline, and politics the partial replication.
